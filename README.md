Hotel Performance & Guest Insights Dashboard

Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze hotel performance across multiple locations in Pakistan, focusing on revenue generation, customer ratings, booking trends, visitor demographics, and amenity usage. The dashboard provides valuable insights for hotel managers and stakeholders to make data-driven decisions to improve occupancy, pricing strategies, and customer satisfaction.

Dataset Used

The dataset contains information on 21 hotels, including:

Hotel Details: Name, location (city, province, coordinates), year, and month.

Performance Metrics: Average occupancy percentage, room rate (PKR), total rooms, rooms booked, total revenue.

Customer Insights: Customer type, rating, foreign vs. local visitors.

Operational Details: Booking source, staff count, amenities count.

Key Questions / KPIs

The dashboard aims to answer:

Which hotels generate the highest total revenue?

What is the distribution of local vs. foreign visitors across cities?

Which provinces and hotels have the highest customer ratings?

What are the most popular booking sources and customer types?

How do amenities correlate with total revenue trends?

Primary KPIs:

Total Revenue (Overall & by hotel)

Total Visitors (Local vs. Foreign)

Average Customer Rating

Revenue by Booking Source

Revenue by Amenities Count

Process

Data Preparation:

Cleaned and standardized hotel names and attributes.

Ensured numeric columns were correctly formatted for aggregation.

Created calculated measures for KPIs such as total visitors and revenue breakdowns.

Data Modeling:

Structured relationships between hotel details, booking info, and customer insights.

Used DAX measures for dynamic calculations.

Dashboard Creation in Power BI:

Designed interactive visuals for each KPI.

Added slicers for hotel name and location filtering.

Applied consistent theme and layout for readability.

Project Insights

Top Performers: Beach Luxury and Marriott dominate in total revenue.

Visitor Trends: Most cities have a balanced mix of local and foreign visitors, but some cities show a clear dominance of one group.

Customer Ratings: Ramada hotels generally receive higher ratings compared to others.

Booking Sources: Booking.com and Travel Agencies contribute the most revenue.

Amenity Impact: Hotels with higher amenities count show a positive trend in total revenue.

Final Conclusion

This dashboard effectively consolidates key hotel performance indicators into a single visual space, enabling decision-makers to:

Identify high and low-performing hotels.

Optimize booking channel strategies.

Improve services in underperforming areas.

Leverage amenity investments for revenue growth.
