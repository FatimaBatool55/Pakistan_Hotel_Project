# Hotel Performance & Guest Insights Dashboard

## Project Objective
Analyze hotel performance across multiple locations in Pakistan, focusing on revenue, customer ratings, booking trends, visitor demographics, and amenity usage. Helps hotel managers make data driven decisions on occupancy, pricing, and customer satisfaction.

## Dataset
Pakistan_Tourism_Hotels.csv: https://github.com/FatimaBatool55/Pakistan_Hotel_Project/blob/main/Pakistan_Tourism_Hotels.csv

Contains data on 21 hotels including name, city, province, coordinates, year, month, occupancy percent, room rate, total rooms, rooms booked, total revenue, customer type, rating, visitor type, booking source, staff count, and amenities count.

## Key Questions
Which hotels generate the highest revenue. Distribution of local versus foreign visitors across cities. Which provinces and hotels have the highest customer ratings. Most popular booking sources and customer types. How amenities correlate with revenue trends.

## Primary KPIs
Total revenue overall and by hotel. Total visitors local versus foreign. Average customer rating by hotel and province. Revenue by booking source. Revenue by amenities count.

## Process
Cleaned and standardized hotel names and attributes, formatted numeric columns, and created calculated measures for KPIs. Modeled relationships between hotel details, booking info, and customer insights using DAX measures. Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with slicers for hotel name and location.

## Key Insights
Beach Luxury and Marriott lead in total revenue. Most cities show a balanced mix of local and foreign visitors. Ramada hotels generally receive higher ratings. Booking.com and Travel Agencies contribute the most revenue. Hotels with more amenities show a positive revenue trend.

## Conclusion
The dashboard consolidates key hotel performance indicators to help identify high and low performing hotels, optimize booking channel strategies, improve services in weaker areas, and guide amenity investments for revenue growth.
